## Cases intentionally not covered

- **W01 on 32-bit targets.** The literal `13835058055282163709` and its overflow test are
  gated on `target_pointer_width = "64"`; on other widths
  `s16_b64_len_domain_matches_checked_div_ceil` still checks the domain boundary generically.
- **W01 overflow without overflow checks.** Release builds wrap instead of panicking (plan §3);
  the `should_panic` tests are gated on `debug_assertions`, the proxy Cargo uses for the default
  of `overflow-checks`. A custom profile with `debug-assertions = true` and
  `overflow-checks = false` would make them fail; the workspace defines none.
- **E10 decoded kid of 256 bytes through `open`.** Unreachable, as the plan says (340 symbols
  decode to at most 255 bytes, and longer segments are rejected first); covered on
  `check_kid_len` and by the existing `byte_identity` case "rs2 kid 256 bytes, encoded 342".
- **Lean-only domains** (plan §3): `component_count` with `Usize.max` dots, transcripts or
  frames of total length above `Usize.max`, and the E07/E08 `with_capacity` sums near
  `usize::MAX`. These inputs cannot be built in Rust (slices are at most `isize::MAX` bytes).
- **SHA-256 bit-counter overflow** for inputs of at least `2^61` bytes (the stated exception of
  the HMAC model): cannot be allocated.
- **Timing.** That E09a tries every fallback and that `verify` is constant time is a timing
  property; functional tests cannot observe it. The match-on-first/middle/last tests and the
  `|=` → `=` mutant show only that the result does not depend on the matching position.
- **Codec states outside `CodecValid`** (an rs1 fallback id that is not a key: the `expect` at
  codec line 652 panics). The constructors prevent it and the specs assume it away (plan §4);
  not tested.
- **Allocation failure.** An explicit assumption of the plan, not a tested behaviour.
