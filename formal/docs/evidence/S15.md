## Design choices

1. **Nested WP with `∀ sealed : Str`.** `seal_rs1`/`seal_rs2` return a Lean `String`, the
   openers take a `Str` (= `Slice U8`). A caller that opens the token passes `&token`
   (`String` → `&str` deref), which is not translated, so no translated conversion exists to
   bind with `do` (unlike `frame_roundtrip`, whose `open_frame` takes `Vec.deref` of the
   frame). Quantifying over every `Str` holding the token's UTF-8 bytes covers any conversion
   and is at least as strong as fixing one. The outer WP also states that the seal terminates
   and, as its first conjunct (decision K1), that such a `Str` exists, so the quantification is
   not empty. No premise is added for it: `h_cap` bounds the token below
   `maxStringBytes ≤ Usize.max`.
2. **Conclusion `resultBytes result = .ok (body.val.drop expiryLen)`**, the plan's `body[8..]`,
   in the same vocabulary as E09–E11 (`resultBytes` is injective, so this fixes `result =
   .Ok v` with `v.val = body[8..]`).
3. **Expiry condition `¬ Expired (bodyExpiry body.val) now_ms.val`** (spec-owner decision (b)),
   the W13 predicate `Defs.Wire.Expired` in the argument form of the incorporated
   `frame_roundtrip` (`h_live`) and of E01's clauses. Equivalent to plan §3's literal
   "expiry is 0 or `now ≤ expiry`" (De Morgan on `e ≠ 0 ∧ n > e`; scratch `notExpired_iff`,
   both directions). The round-1 draft used the literal disjunction.
4. **One rs1 theorem with a disjunctive key condition** rather than two (single key / ring): the
   plan fixes one name per round trip (`rs1_roundtrip`, `open_at_roundtrip_rs1`), and its key
   condition is itself a disjunction. Each disjunct is the weakest structural form:
   single key: the key bytes equal the seal key bytes; ring: some rs1 fallback id whose
   `lookupKey` (model of `keys.get`) is the seal key bytes. No requirement that the seal mode
   names that id, or that the seal mode is rs1 at all (`open_rs1_at` never reads it); spec-owner
   decision F9, recorded in `formal/docs/evidence/S13.md`, "Spec-owner decisions (2026-11-23)"
   ("no 'rs1 seal kid ∈ fallbacks' clause").
5. **Codec premise `CodecFallbacksKnown self`, not `CodecValid self`.** The style guide allows
   `CodecValid` for composition theorems, but only the fallback clause is used, and it is
   necessary (scratch evaluation: the codec `ringBadFb` panics on all 256 of its rs1 cases).
   `CodecValid` would also make `h_kid_len` derivable for rs2 (every key id of a valid ring
   passes E04), but would add premises (distinct ids, key lengths) the round trip does not need.
6. **No extra domain premise.** E09/E10's `h_count` and `h_len` and E11's `h_len1`/`h_len2` are
   discharged at the sealed token from E07/E08's `h_len` (the scratch proofs confirm it).
7. **No new `Defs`.** The statements use only existing definitions (`lookupKey`,
   `CodecFallbacksKnown`, `resultBytes`, `bodyExpiry`, `Expired`, `expiryLen`, `maxKidLen`, `b64Len`,
   `transcriptV1/V2`, `versionV1/V2`, `ExtModel.utf8`).
8. **Names in `rmcp.model.request_state.RequestStateCodec`**, by analogy with `frame_roundtrip`
   (`rmcp.model.request_state_wire`, the namespace of the functions it composes); the composed
   functions are associated functions of `RequestStateCodec`.

## Decisions

Spec-owner (human) decisions of 2026-11-23 on the round-1 open questions:

- (a) **Conclusion form: kept plan-literal.** `resultBytes result = .ok (body.val.drop expiryLen)`
  under `h_body` and `h_expiry`. The stronger `resultBytes result = openBody body.val now_ms.val`
  (premises dropped) is not adopted.
- (b) **Expiry premise: `¬ Expired (bodyExpiry body.val) now_ms.val`** in all four theorems,
  matching `frame_roundtrip` (`Specs/Model/RequestStateWire.lean`) and E01
  (`Specs/Model/RequestState.lean`). Docstrings updated: `Expired e n := e ≠ 0 ∧ n > e` models
  W13 `is_expired` (`request_state_wire.rs` lines 209–212), and `¬ Expired e n ↔ e = 0 ∨ n ≤ e`
  is plan §3's second condition. Applied in the draft; nothing else in the statements changed.
- (c) **Negative round trips: deferred, out of scope for S15.** A token sealed under another key,
  or tampered, being rejected with `IntegrityCheckFailed` is not in the plan; with the opaque
  HMAC model it is provable only under a no-collision premise such as
  `hmacSha256 key m ≠ hmacSha256 key' m`. Not an open question of S15.

## Open questions

None. Round-1 questions (a) and (b) are decided, (c) is deferred (section "Decisions"). Their
round-1 text, for the record:

- (a) Conclusion form: the plan-literal `resultBytes result = .ok (body.val.drop expiryLen)`
  under `h_body`/`h_expiry` (drafted), or the strictly stronger
  `resultBytes result = openBody body.val now_ms.val` with `h_body`/`h_expiry` dropped, which
  also pins the `MalformedFormat`/`Expired` results of a round trip with a short or expired
  body (the scratch helpers `openRs1_roundtrip`/`openRs2_roundtrip` pass through it).
- (b) Expiry premise: literal plan disjunction (drafted) or `¬ Expired (bodyExpiry body.val)
  now_ms.val` as in `frame_roundtrip` (equivalent).
- (c) Negative round trips (a token sealed under another key, or tampered, is rejected with
  `IntegrityCheckFailed`): not in the plan. With the opaque HMAC model they are provable only
  under a premise such as `hmacSha256 key m ≠ hmacSha256 key' m` (no collision), since the model
  says nothing about distinct keys. Out of scope unless the spec owner adds them.
  (Decided 2026-11-23: deferred.)
