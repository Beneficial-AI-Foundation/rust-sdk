## For later steps

(a) **Domain premises of the E specs (S05 later batches, S13).** E09–E11 and the round trips
cannot discharge the W10/W11 `h_len` or the W06/W08/W09 `h_count` premise from what they know:
`b64Decode` is opaque and unbounded, and `Slice` gives only count ≤ length ≤ `Usize.max`. The E
specs need their own domain premise, either on `b64Decode` of the parsed parts or via an extra
Base64 length law (`(b64Decode x).length ≤ x.length`) plus a premise on the token and `ad`. The
spec owner decides when those specs are drafted.

(b) **S08 requirement.** A URL-safe alphabet law for `b64`: its output bytes lie in
`[A-Za-z0-9_-]` (or at least contain no `.`), backed by Rust test vectors. S15 needs it to apply
W08/W09 to sealed tokens.

(c) **Planned `Proofs` lemmas.** `(transcriptV1 ad body).length = 34 + ad.length + body.length`;
the V2 analogue `(transcriptV2 kid ad body).length = 42 + kid.length + ad.length + body.length`;
`(frameBytes e p).length = 8 + p.length`; and `bodyExpiry (frameBytes e p) = e` for `e` in the
`i64` range (needed by `frame_roundtrip`).

(d) **Decisions for the spec owner.** Whether the W01 lemma `b64Len n = n.div_ceil 3 * 4` (`Nat`
ceiling) is drafted in S05 or left to `Proofs`; equality versus `match` form of the
postcondition (W03, W07, W08, W09, W14, E01, E03, E04).

- E01: bodies of 0–7 bytes; exactly 8 bytes with expiry 0; expiry 10 with `now` 9, 10, 11;
