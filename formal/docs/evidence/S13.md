## Stated exceptions

HMAC SHA-256 always succeeds in the model, but its bit-length counter overflows for messages of at
least 2^61 − 64 bytes or keys of at least 2^61 bytes; allocation failure is not modelled; the
transcript `Vec` is modelled up to `Usize.max` bytes. These inputs cannot occur on the pinned
target's address space.
