## Stated exceptions of the S08 models (not modelled)

- `hmac_sha256`, `hmac_sha256_verify`: SHA-256 finalisation computes the bit length as a `u64`
  (`sha2-0.11.1/src/block_api.rs` line 66), which overflows, and panics under `overflow-checks`,
  for a message of at least `2^61 − 64` bytes and for a key of at least `2^61` bytes (hashed by
  `get_der_key`). Outside the size convention defined in the module docstring of
  `FunsExternal.lean` (no claim about inputs of at least `2^61` bytes; unreachable on `x86_64`,
  whose virtual address space is at most `2^57` bytes).
- `hmac_sha256_verify`: constant-time comparison (timing is not modelled).
- `b64_encode_into`: `ok` from a `String` model means "returns, or aborts or panics for memory
  reasons" (out of memory; `RawVec::grow_amortized` past `isize::MAX` once the capacity exceeds
  `isize::MAX / 2`); growth of the length beyond `isize::MAX` is `fail panic`.
